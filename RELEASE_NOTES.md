## What's new in 0.7.0

- **Nova now lives inside Vantage.** A new Nova tab with four chats, like a messenger: Nova (ask anything), Raka (your profile and experience), the Applications group (Sinta, Arga, Bayu and Tari: job search, fit check, CV and cover letter, document check) and Laras (interview practice). You no longer need Claude Desktop open or a chat pinned.
- **Connect Claude once.** Vantage installs Claude Code with Anthropic's official installer and you sign in on Claude's own page with your Claude Pro or Max plan. Vantage never sees your account.
- **Tidy up stays on the Inbox page.** Raka decides where each file goes, Vantage moves it, and a message from Raka tells you what went where.
- **You see what each reply costs.** Every answer shows its share of your 5-hour and weekly limits and who on the team worked on it; the chat list shows your current limits.
- **Much lighter on your plan.** Small talk gets a quick, cheap answer; real work runs on the stronger model. Vantage no longer loads your other Claude connectors, plugins or unused tools, and starts each chat with your status already loaded.
- The old Chat tab (claude.ai inside Vantage) is gone; buttons such as Update Master CV, Prepare interview and the morning report now open the right chat with the request ready to send.
